FAQ

How do I register my organization?

Simply click on “Register” at the top of the page and fill out your organization’s name and admin details. Once submitted, your unique workspace will be created automatically.

How do staff join?

Organization admins can send invites to staff via email. Once invited, staff can complete their setup and securely join the team workspace.

Can we manage multiple buildings?

Yes. Gida supports multi-building operations. Staff can switch between buildings from their dashboard to view or update location-specific data.

Is there a separate admin view?

Absolutely. Admins have full control to manage users, update organization settings like logos and addresses, and view activity across shifts and buildings.

What happens at the end of a shift?

At the end of each shift, Gida compiles the round logs, client status, passes, and any incidents into a shift report that can be saved or printed.

Can I track client absences and passes?

Yes. Staff can mark absences, track time out/in, and record valid passes—all of which appear in reports and client history.

Is Gida available 24/7?

Gida is built to support 24/7 operations across 3 daily shifts, ensuring no activity is missed and every entry is securely stored in real time.
